# Contact Us

**EDUCARD TECHNOLOGIES**

We are here to help with questions about EduCard, account access, school-management features, technical issues, and privacy-related concerns.

If you use EduCard as a school administrator, teacher, or parent, please contact us using the details below.

## General Support

For assistance with EduCard, account-related questions, technical issues, or feedback, contact:

**Email:** support@educard.info  
**Website:** https://educard.info

Please describe your issue clearly so that our support team can understand your request and provide appropriate assistance.

## Account Deletion Requests

### Administrator Accounts

Administrators who want to delete their EduCard accounts can email support@educard.info.

Please use the subject line: **EduCard Account Deletion Request**.

Include your registered email address, organization name, and account role. We may request additional information to verify your identity before processing the request.

### Teacher and Parent Accounts

Teacher and Parent accounts are managed by the Organization Admin.

Please contact your school's or organization's Organization Admin to request account deletion. If you cannot contact your Organization Admin, email support@educard.info for assistance.

For additional information, visit:

https://educard.info/account-deletion

## Privacy and Data Protection

For questions about personal information, data retention, privacy rights, or the handling of student information, please email support@educard.info.

Please do not include passwords, authentication codes, or unnecessary sensitive student information in your email.

You can review our Privacy Policy here:

https://educard.info/privacy-policy

## Technical Support

When reporting a technical issue, please provide a description of the problem, the affected feature, and any relevant error message. Avoid sending confidential information unless it is specifically necessary and requested through an appropriate channel.

Visit https://educard.info to learn more about EduCard and access our published policies.
