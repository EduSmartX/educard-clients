# EduCard Account Deletion

**Operated by EDUCARD TECHNOLOGIES**  
**Last Updated:** October 12, 2026

EduCard respects your privacy and provides a process for requesting account deletion. The procedure depends on your account type and the organization associated with your account.

## 1. How to Request Account Deletion

### Administrator Accounts

If you are an administrator who wants to delete your EduCard account, follow these steps:

1. Send an email to **support@educard.info**.
2. Use the subject line **EduCard Account Deletion Request**.
3. Include your registered email address, organization name, and account role.
4. Clearly state that you are requesting deletion of your EduCard account.

We may contact you to verify your identity and authorization before processing the request.

### Teacher and Parent Accounts

Teacher and Parent accounts are managed by the Organization Admin.

To request deletion:

1. Contact your school's or organization's Organization Admin.
2. Ask the Organization Admin to delete your EduCard account.
3. If you cannot contact your Organization Admin or need assistance, email **support@educard.info**.

The Organization Admin is responsible for managing teacher and parent accounts within the organization.

## 2. What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted?

EduCard's current account-deletion scope is account-only.

When an account is deleted:

- Access to the deleted account is removed.
- The account can no longer be used to sign in.
- Personal information associated with the account is handled according to the applicable data-retention and deletion requirements.
- School or organizational records may remain where necessary for legitimate educational, administrative, accounting, or legal purposes.

Deleting a user's account does not automatically delete every record associated with that user. For example, attendance, academic, homework, or fee records may need to remain available to the school or organization.

Information that is no longer required will be deleted or anonymized where appropriate, subject to applicable retention requirements.

## 3. Data Retention After Account Deletion

Personal information retained in our systems following account deletion is retained for **up to 90 days after account deletion**, unless a longer period is required or permitted under applicable legal or legitimate retention requirements.

Once the applicable retention period ends, information will be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

Certain school or organizational records may be retained separately by the relevant school or organization under its applicable record-retention requirements. Such records are not necessarily removed when an individual account is deleted.

## 4. Verification and Processing

We may verify the identity and authority of the person submitting a deletion request to prevent unauthorized deletion.

The time required to process a request may depend on verification, the account type, the Organization Admin's involvement, and any applicable retention obligations.

If additional information is required, we will contact the requester using the information provided.
